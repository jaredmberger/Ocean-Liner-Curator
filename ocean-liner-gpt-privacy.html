Ocean Liner GPT Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026 · Ocean Liner Curator LLC

Ocean Liner GPT is published by Ocean Liner Curator LLC. This policy describes information received by the publisher in connection with the plugin. It does not replace OpenAI’s privacy policy or account settings.

Conversations and uploads

Ocean Liner GPT provides instructions and reference materials within OpenAI’s services. The plugin does not send your conversations or uploaded files to systems operated by Ocean Liner Curator LLC. The publisher does not receive those conversations or uploads through a publisher-operated plugin server.

OpenAI processes information you provide to its services under its own policies, terms, and account controls. Consult those policies and controls for information about platform storage, use, retention, and deletion. Do not include confidential or sensitive information unnecessarily.

Voluntary support correspondence

If you email curator@oceanliners.net, you voluntarily provide your email address, message, and any attachments or other information included in the correspondence. Support correspondence is used only to respond to support requests and is periodically deleted. No fixed deletion interval is specified. That correspondence is separate from your ChatGPT conversation. Send only the information needed to explain your question, and remove unrelated personal or sensitive information.

For privacy questions concerning information you have sent directly to Ocean Liner Curator LLC, contact curator@oceanliners.net. Questions about information held by OpenAI should be directed to OpenAI through its privacy and account tools.

Scope

This policy concerns Ocean Liner GPT. Visiting OceanLiners.net or using other services may involve separate data practices. If a future plugin version adds a publisher-operated service or changes its data flow, this policy will be updated to describe that behavior.
