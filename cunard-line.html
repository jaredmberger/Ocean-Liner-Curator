Why this hub?

Cunard as a long Atlantic system

Cunard is not just a handful of famous ships. It is a long company story about schedule reliability, mail service, speed, national prestige, wartime mobilization, preservation, and the changing meaning of ocean-liner travel.

Schedule and reliability first

Cunard’s early importance begins with regularized transatlantic service. That emphasis on dependable crossings shaped the company long before the famous four-funnel express era.
