SS President Lincoln

Hamburg-America Line · entered service 1907 · Ship Guide

Overview

SS President Lincoln had an unusually complicated origin. Harland & Wolff launched the hull on 8 October 1903 as Scotian for Furness, Withy & Co., but HAPAG acquired the unfinished ship before completion. She finally entered service in 1907 under the name President Lincoln.

Her civilian career centered on Hamburg–New York passenger service. After the United States entered the First World War, the interned German liner was seized, repaired after deliberate disabling by her crew, and commissioned as a U.S. Navy troop transport. U-90 torpedoed and sank her on 31 May 1918.

From Scotian to President Lincoln

NHHC and GG Archives agree on the key point: the ship did not originate as a completed HAPAG liner. The hull was launched as Scotian and acquired before completion. NHHC additionally notes an intermediate proposed name, Berlin, before President Lincoln was adopted.

That pre-service history is exactly the kind of name sequence that can create catalog errors if only the final operating identity is retained.

HAPAG Passenger Service

GG Archives preserves 1910 passenger lists showing President Lincoln on Hamburg–New York service via ports including Southampton and Boulogne-sur-Mer. Her very large third-class capacity reflected HAPAG's continuing importance in the European emigrant trade.

U.S. Troop Transport

After seizure in New York in 1917, the ship required substantial repair before commissioning. NHHC records multiple eastbound troop voyages carrying American Expeditionary Force personnel to France.

31 May 1918

On her fifth return voyage, President Lincoln was attacked by U-90 and sank after torpedo hits. NHHC records 26 deaths. Hundreds of survivors remained in boats and rafts until destroyers USS Warrington and USS Smith reached the area.

Interpretive Notes

Scotian, HAPAG President Lincoln, and USS President Lincoln represent successive documentary identities. The hull continuity is real, but the operational context changed completely.
