SS Persic

White Star Line · entered service 1899 · Ship Guide

Overview

SS Persic was a Jubilee-class White Star cargo-passenger liner built by Harland & Wolff for the Australia trade. Launched on 7 September 1899 and entering service late that year, she combined substantial refrigerated cargo capacity with passenger accommodation on the Liverpool–Cape Town–Australia route.

A Working White Star Liner

Persic illustrates the commercial breadth of White Star far better than a prestige-only fleet narrative. Passenger movement, military transport and refrigerated cargo all belonged to the same hull history.

Australia Route

GG Archives identifies Persic as an England–Australia ship via Cape Town and preserves a 1910 third-class passage contract for travel within Australia. White Star's 1907 line brochure, however, marketed the wider Australia service as carrying one class only—evidence that terminology and onboard classification could vary by document and date.

War Service

During the First World War Persic served in transport roles. In 1918 she was torpedoed but remained afloat and was saved, later returning to commercial work.

Interpretive Notes

Passenger-class labels should follow the document being described rather than be normalized too aggressively. Company promotional language and surviving ticket terminology do not always use identical categories.
