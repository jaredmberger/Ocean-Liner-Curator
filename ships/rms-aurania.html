RMS Aurania (1916)

Cunard Line · completed 1917 · Ship Guide

Overview

RMS Aurania is unusual among Cunard liners because her intended peacetime identity never really happened. Ordered before the First World War for Canada–Europe service, she was delayed, launched on 16 July 1916, completed in March 1917 and went directly into government troop transport.

Designed for Peace, Completed for War

Aurania should not be described as if she accumulated a normal Cunard passenger career. Her civilian route existed as design intent; the actual documentary life of the ship is overwhelmingly military.

UB-67 Attack

uboat.net's February 1918 loss record identifies UB-67 as the attacker. The damaged ship was taken in tow but later stranded near the Isle of Mull and broke up in heavy weather.

Interpretive Notes

This distinction between intended and actual service is crucial for provenance. Passenger publicity describing the planned ship and wartime records describing the operating vessel belong to different evidentiary categories.
