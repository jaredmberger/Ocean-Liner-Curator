SS Baltic (1871)

White Star Line · entered service 1871 · Ship Guide

Overview

SS Baltic was one of White Star's first-generation Oceanic-class liners. Harland & Wolff launched her on 8 March 1871 under the intended name Pacific; she entered service instead as Baltic on 14 September 1871.

She quickly demonstrated that White Star's comfort-oriented design philosophy did not exclude competitive speed. Norway-Heritage records a January 1873 westbound record passage that placed the ship in the Blue Riband tradition of the period.

Pacific Becomes Baltic

The launch name is a useful provenance trap. Material documenting the completed passenger liner belongs to Baltic; “Pacific” applies to the pre-service construction phase.

Record Service

Norway-Heritage records Baltic gaining the Blue Riband after a January 1873 New York–Queenstown passage. The claim should remain tied to that specific route and date rather than expanded into a timeless “fastest liner” label.

Veendam

After later Inman charters, Holland America purchased the ship in 1888 and renamed her Veendam. This creates a hard operator and livery boundary even though the hull was unchanged.

Interpretive Notes

White Star Baltic and Holland America Veendam should be treated as separate catalog identities. This 1871 ship must also be distinguished from White Star's much larger 1904 Baltic.
