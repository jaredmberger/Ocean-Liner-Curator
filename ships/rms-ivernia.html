RMS Ivernia (1955)

Cunard Line · entered service 1955 · Ship Guide

Overview

RMS Ivernia was the second of Cunard's four postwar Canadian-service sisters. John Brown launched her at Clydebank on 14 December 1954, and she entered service on 1 July 1955 from Greenock to Quebec and Montreal.

The Canadian Quartet

Ivernia belonged with Saxonia, Carinthia and Sylvania in Cunard's effort to renew medium-sized postwar service. Their tourist-heavy capacity shows how sharply the passenger market had changed from the prewar first-class model.

Franconia

After rebuilding, Ivernia became Franconia in 1963 and increasingly served the cruise market. Specifications from the two identities should not be merged casually; tonnage, accommodation and visual appearance changed.

Interpretive Notes

The later Fedor Shalyapin phase extended the hull's life for decades, but belongs to a different operator and cultural context. Use the printed ship name as the primary provenance anchor.
