SS Arabic (1921)

White Star Line · entered White Star service 1921 · Ship Guide

Overview

White Star's second SS Arabic was the former Norddeutscher Lloyd liner Berlin, built by AG Weser and completed in 1909. After the First World War she was ceded to Britain, purchased by White Star in November 1920, refitted, and entered White Star service in September 1921.

Her postwar career moved among Southampton–New York, Mediterranean service and a later Red Star charter. That multi-line history makes her one of the clearest examples of postwar tonnage redistribution and name reuse.

Berlin Becomes Arabic

The ship's German origin is not a side note—it is the organizing fact of her White Star career. Postwar redistribution turned a prewar NDL liner into a British fleet replacement during a period of severe tonnage disruption.

Mediterranean and Red Star Service

GG Archives schedules place the later Arabic alongside Adriatic and Lapland on Mediterranean service in the early 1920s, and later company material records the ship in Red Star-associated service. One hull therefore generated authentic ephemera under more than one line identity.

The Arabic Name Trap

The earlier White Star Arabic was sunk in 1915. Any artifact dated 1921–1931 belongs to the ex-Berlin ship instead. Route, date and company imprint should always accompany the name where possible.

Interpretive Notes

This guide titles the ship “Arabic (1921)” to reflect actual entry into White Star service, while preserving the existing /ss-arabic-1920 URL for continuity.
