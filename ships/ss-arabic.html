SS Arabic (1903)

White Star Line · entered service 1903 · Ship Guide

Overview

SS Arabic was a White Star intermediate liner completed in 1903 after having originally been intended for Atlantic Transport Line as Minnewaska. The transfer before completion places her squarely inside IMM's shared fleet-development world.

She entered Liverpool–New York service in June 1903 and remained a practical multi-class Atlantic liner until U-24 torpedoed and sank her on 19 August 1915.

From Minnewaska to Arabic

The ship's pre-completion transfer is an important cataloging fact. A design intended for Atlantic Transport entered service instead as a White Star liner, showing how IMM's corporate structure could redirect new tonnage before a ship even made a voyage.

The 1915 Sinking

GG Archives records the loss off southern Ireland on 19 August 1915 with 44 deaths, while uboat.net identifies the attacker as U-24 under Rudolf Schneider. The incident became part of the wider diplomatic controversy over German submarine warfare and American neutrality.

Interpretive Notes

This is the first White Star Arabic. It must be distinguished from the former NDL Berlin, which entered White Star service as another Arabic after World War I.
