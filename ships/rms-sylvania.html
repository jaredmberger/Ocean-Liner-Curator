RMS Sylvania (1957)

Cunard Line · entered service 1957 · Ship Guide

Overview

RMS Sylvania was the fourth and final member of Cunard's postwar Canadian-service quartet. Launched by John Brown on 31 October 1956 and entering service on 5 June 1957, she arrived at the very end of the traditional liner era.

The Last Sister

By the time Sylvania entered service, the economics of ocean travel were already beginning to change. Her large tourist-class complement and flexible seasonal employment capture Cunard's attempt to keep regular liner service commercially relevant.

Fairwind and Beyond

After leaving Cunard in 1968, the ship became Fairwind and developed a long cruise career. Later identities such as Dawn Princess and Albatros belong to separate brands, refits and passenger markets.

Interpretive Notes

Sylvania is an unusually good case study in hull continuity versus identity change. The same structure served multiple travel cultures over nearly half a century, but the documentary contexts should not be collapsed into one.
