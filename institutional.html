Our Purpose

Founded on 15 December 2025, Ocean Liner Curator LLC develops a freely accessible public resource on the ships, people, systems and material culture of ocean travel. Our scope includes individual vessels and the wider technological, social, economic and cultural history in which they operated.

We seek to make historical knowledge accessible without removing the uncertainty, attribution and context that responsible interpretation requires.

Research & Historical Integrity

Our editorial approach favors identifiable evidence, careful source comparison and attribution that is proportionate to the record. Conflicting dates, uncertain identifications and unsupported traditions are described as such rather than resolved through speculation.

  • Evidence before certainty. Claims are evaluated against available documentation, with preference for primary and archival sources where appropriate.
  • Transparent uncertainty. When evidence is incomplete or contradictory, limitations are disclosed and conclusions restrained.
  • Accountable revision. Research leads can prompt further review, but a proposal is not automatically a verified historical correction.
  • Human editorial judgment. AI-based tools may assist research and organization; they are not treated as independent historical authorities.

Governance & Stewardship

Ocean Liner Curator LLC is guided by principles of historical integrity, education, independence, collaboration, humility and continuity. Stewardship means preserving provenance, acknowledging what is not known and correcting errors when justified by evidence.

Public access to our educational resources is free and the website does not carry on-site advertising. Our work is independent of the organizations and collections it references.

Institutional Engagement

We welcome dialogue with maritime museums, archives, historical societies, preservation organizations, educational institutions and independent researchers. Possible areas of shared interest include source identification, research exchange, educational resource development and referrals to complementary collections.

Requests to reproduce materials, exchange research or discuss prospective initiatives should be considered individually, with due attention to rights, attribution and institutional responsibilities. No formal partnership or endorsement should be inferred from a correspondence or external citation.

What we offer
