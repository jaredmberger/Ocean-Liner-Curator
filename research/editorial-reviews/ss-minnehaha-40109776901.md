## Claim 1 — conflicting launch dates
**Decision: needs_research; no guide change.** The Titanic Inquiry Project ship note gives 25 March 1900; TheYard.info Harland & Wolff yard-list compilation gives 31 March 1900 for yard no. 329. This is a conflict among secondary sources, **not** a verified correction. The guide's existing 31 March date remains provisional. Seek an original Harland & Wolff launch record or contemporary Belfast press notice before changing it.

- https://www.titanicinquiry.org/ships/minnehaha.php
- https://www.theyard.info/ (yard-list lead; primary yard record not yet checked)

## Claim 2 — 7 July 1915 explosion and Halifax diversion
**Decision: verified for date/event/diversion; guide clarification approved.** The *San Antonio Light* of 8 July 1915 reports an onboard explosion and fire on 7 July, with Captain Claret reporting control of the fire while making for Halifax. It links the episode to contemporary suspicion around Frank Holt (Erich Muenter), **not** a formal attribution or definitive sabotage finding. The prior guide already described the episode substantially correctly. The guide change specifies the day and no longer implies the fire was first brought under control in Halifax.

- Contemporary source: https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth1601155/m1/1/
- Additional existing guide source: https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/title/commercial-financial-chronicle-1339/july-17-1915-497324/fulltext

## Claim 3 — reported sinking coordinates
**Decision: needs_research; no guide change.** The memorial compilation supplies approximately 51°17′N 09°22′W, while another wreck listing supplies a different position. Do not describe a reported casualty position as the present wreck location, or publish precise coordinates without primary navigation/casualty evidence. Consult Admiralty wartime casualty returns, Lloyd's War Losses, and, if available, U-48's patrol/war diary; determine whether coordinates refer to attack, sinking, or wreck location.

- https://www.benjidog.co.uk/Tower%20Hill/WW1%20Minnehaha%20to%20Moeris.php
