Suggestions & Corrections

Ocean Liner Curator is built as an evolving reference archive. If you noticed an error, found a missing link, know of a useful source, or have an idea for a future page, you can send it here.

What to send

The most useful notes are specific: a ship name, page URL, source title, date, publication, archive reference, or a short explanation of what should be changed.

  • Corrections to facts, dates, names, captions, or links
  • Ideas for future ship guides or research pages
  • Sources, citations, photos, catalog records, or archival leads
  • Pages that were hard to find or topics you expected to see
  • Accessibility, navigation, or design issues

Submissions are reviewed manually. A suggestion may inform a future update, but not every submission will receive a direct reply.

Send a suggestion

Include enough context to verify the note: page, ship, source, date, or the exact text involved.

Your message will be sent through Formspree. Please avoid submitting private or sensitive personal information.
