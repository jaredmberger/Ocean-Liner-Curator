Support Ocean Liner Curator

Ocean Liner Curator is a reader-supported maritime history project devoted to evidence-first research, careful interpretation, object documentation, and free public access to ocean liner history.

⁂ Funding principle: Support should sustain the mission without changing the mission. Contributions help keep the project maintained, independent, and publicly available while preserving its evidence-first editorial standards.

Why Support the Project?

Ocean Liner Curator is built as a public reference project: ship guides, research essays, object records, source pages, historical explainers, and interpretive material made available without paywalls. Voluntary support helps cover the practical costs of keeping that work online, organized, and growing.

Ocean liner history deserves careful treatment. The subject is often beautiful, nostalgic, and dramatic, but the work behind it is patient: checking sources, comparing claims, documenting uncertainty, preserving images and objects, and presenting history in a form readers can trust.

Support Is Voluntary

Support is never required to use Ocean Liner Curator. The site is intended to remain freely accessible to readers, collectors, researchers, students, family historians, and anyone interested in the world of ocean liners.

A contribution is simply a way to help sustain the work: the quiet, ongoing labor of maintaining pages, improving references, documenting objects, and preserving a careful record of maritime history.

What Support Does Not Buy

Contributions do not purchase editorial influence, historical conclusions, object authentication, appraisal opinions, or preferential treatment. Ocean Liner Curator’s research standards remain the same whether a page is popular, obscure, supported, or unsupported.

Claims are still weighed against evidence. Uncertainty is still marked. Attribution remains careful. The project’s purpose is stewardship, not promotion.

Editorial Independence

  • Evidence first: Reader support helps maintain the project, but it does not determine what the evidence says.
  • No paywall: Support is voluntary and is not intended to restrict public access to the core reference material.
  • No exaggerated claims: Financial support does not change the project’s preference for restraint, sourcing, and transparent limits.
  • No advertising pressure: Reader support helps reduce the need for intrusive advertising or content shaped mainly by traffic incentives.
  • Mission before monetization: Funding exists to support the work, not to redefine it.

A Curatorial Standard

Ocean Liner Curator treats ships, documents, photographs, menus, passenger lists, tableware, souvenirs, and memories as evidence deserving patience and context. Support helps make that careful work more sustainable.

Guiding standard: Preserve what can be preserved. Explain what can be explained. Mark uncertainty where it remains. Keep ocean liner history accessible, careful, and alive for future readers.
