# Ship guide audit — five-guide batch (11 October 2026)

Scope: current repository HTML guides, preservation-first review of history, sourcing, structure, and canonical metadata. This is a *review record*, not a claim of comprehensive external verification. Existing narrative and historical detail are preserved; further claims should be independently verified before edits.

| Guide | Findings and disposition |
| --- | --- |
| `ships/ms-oranje.html` | Full career treatment retained: 1939 East Indies service, hospital-ship years, 1953 collision, Angelina Lauro phase. Wartime role aligns broadly with the Australian War Memorial's Oranje account. Different tonnage conventions need continued source attribution, not forced harmonization. No edit. |
| `ships/ms-willem-ruys.html` | Wartime construction delay, Royal Rotterdam Lloyd service, collision with Oranje, and Achille Lauro career are distinguished. 2 December 1947 maiden-departure wording has a specifically cited publisher/source basis in the guide; competing later secondary summaries give 5 December, so preserve source-attributed chronology pending documentary reconciliation. No edit. |
| `ships/ms-pilsudski.html` | Guide appropriately distinguishes the likely mine explanation from proof of the November 1939 sinking. Independent Piłsudski history material also describes the cause as unresolved. Preserve this uncertainty. No edit. |
| `ships/rms-teutonic.html` | Core 1889 dates, first White Star twin-screw significance, and Majestic relationship consistent with historical White Star references. The sister link was separately repaired in PR #391 to `/ships/ss-majestic-1889`. No further edit. |
| `ships/ss-cap-polonio.html` | Key Facts repeated yard no. 221 in both Builder and a standalone Yard number row. Remove redundant row only; preserve all historical narrative, numerical values, and bibliography. Existing source list is dominated by Wikipedia and deserves archival strengthening in a future research-led pass, not unsourced rewriting. |
