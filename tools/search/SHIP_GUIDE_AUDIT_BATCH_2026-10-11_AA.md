# Ship-guide sourcing audit — batch 37 (11 October 2026)

A preservation-first review of five existing ship guides and the specific sourcing choices visible in their current text. This is a **limited editorial assessment of repository content**, not fresh inspection of all linked archive documents, an independent fact-check of every number, or an external URL health crawl. It must not be counted as full-guide verification.

| Guide | Focus and existing evidence | Disposition / remaining work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ships/ss-president-harding.html` | Original *Lone Star State*, transitional *President Taft*, interwar *President Harding* and final *Ville de Bruges* identity sequence. The guide cites E. C. McKellar's U.S. Shipping Board construction study and the ship-specific Shipscribe Design 1029 photographic material. | Retain the carefully qualified May 1940 loss description; do not collapse bombing, beaching, burning and sinking into one unqualified event without a contemporaneous casualty or Belgian port record. Further work: original 1940 casualty notice and transfer registry. |
| `ships/ss-orion.html` | *Orion* of the Orient Line, distinguished from a German commerce raider of the same name. Existing guide includes Lloyd's Register Foundation material and Australian War Memorial photographic collection citations; its interpretive discussion separates prewar, wartime, postwar and one-class service configurations. | Retain configuration distinctions and source attributions. A dated Orient Line accommodation plan or refurbishment specification is needed before reconciling phase-specific passenger numbers. No narrative edits. |
| `ships/ms-felix-roussel.html` | Messageries Maritimes specialist ship account addresses launch and entry into service, 1935 alteration, troopship service and eventual later identity. The guide already distinguishes rebuilt dimensions from as-built figures. | Retain; the specialist compilation is not itself a substitute for original Saint-Nazaire yard drawings, ship registers, voyage orders or disposal records. No unqualified numerical correction. |
| `ships/ss-marechal-joffre.html` | The source set distinguishes French Messageries Maritimes history from U.S. Navy service as USS *Rochambeau* (AP-63), including Naval History and Heritage Command DANFS and Shipscribe naval auxiliary records. | Retain the French/U.S. service phases separately. Future check: compare original Navy conversion/commission documents and postwar French return/registry documents rather than treating one phase's technical figures as universal. |
| `ships/rms-rangitane.html` | National Library of New Zealand catalogue, NZ History ship-specific account and Merchant Navy timeline, and an April 1941 contemporary newspaper reference underpin wartime fate narrative. The guide explicitly notes differing 15 and 16 fatality counts in NZ History materials. | Preserve the casualty disagreement. Do not choose a definitive number without original passenger/crew manifests and casualty return(s). Distinguish this merchant liner from the auxiliary cruiser *Orion* implicated in the 1940 attack. |

**Outcome:** All five guides preserved unchanged. No newly verified factual correction justified from this repository-level sampling. No Key Facts, historical paragraphs, titles, media, navigation or metadata are modified. Recording these evidence gaps makes later targeted original-source research more useful than speculative rewriting.
