## Checks and editorial limits

- Exactly one new link to the collection verified in each changed branch guide.
- The destination is an existing research collection, and additions are adjacent to passages concerning port or transfer infrastructure.
- Original ship facts, chronology, sources, identity boundaries and metadata have not been rewritten.
- RMS Queen Mary was excluded because it already contains a link to this destination (with `.html` suffix); other guides with incidental port mentions were not forced into the batch.
