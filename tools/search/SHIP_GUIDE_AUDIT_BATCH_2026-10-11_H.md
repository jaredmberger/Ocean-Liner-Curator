# Ship guide audit — batch 18 (11 October 2026)

Five distinct guides reviewed for evidence quality, phase-specific chronology, primary-source traceability, and preservation: `rms-celtic.html`, `rms-cedric.html`, `rms-adriatic.html`, `rms-baltic.html`, and `ss-majestic-1889.html` (all under `ships/`).

| Guide | Finding | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RMS *Celtic* (1901) | National Museums NI's Harland & Wolff photographic catalogue explicitly anchors yard no. 335, launch, and handover. The guide appropriately distinguishes alternate 25/26 July maiden-voyage date conventions. | Preserve existing figures and chronology. |
| RMS *Cedric* (1903) | Titanic Belfast's ship fact file provides an institutional check; February 1903 newspaper *transcriptions* are labeled as such rather than misrepresented as directly consulted original print. | Preserve; original newspaper issue would be worthwhile further primary-source work. |
| RMS *Adriatic* (1907) | Chronology and shipyard context cite Titanic Belfast and The Yard. Original design, wartime requisition, and later interwar operations are distinguished. | Preserve; additional museum/registry documentation could deepen evidence. |
| RMS *Baltic* (1904) | Titanic Belfast and White Star History support the ship overview; U.S. Institute for Telecommunication Sciences' January 1909 rescue history supports the *Republic* wireless-response context. | Preserve; no unsupported embellishment of rescue role. |
| SS *Majestic* (1889) | Its contemporary 1891 *Ocean Steamships* reference provides a period source for Atlantic passage comparisons. This vessel's canonical guide is `/ships/ss-majestic-1889`, distinct from later similarly named ships. | Preserve distinctions and route identity. |

**Disposition:** No factual corrections or numerical overwrites were warranted by this limited review. These five guides receive a documentation-only audit record. This does not certify every claim, the state of external links/images, or the completeness of historical bibliography. Seek primary documents when a specific unresolved claim justifies further work.
