# Ship-guide sourcing audit — batch 35 (11 October 2026)

Five distinct guides reviewed: `ships/ss-iberia.html`, `ships/ss-champlain.html`, `ships/ss-lafayette.html`, `ships/ss-france.html` (1962), and `ships/ss-cap-arcona.html`.

Scope: preservation-first review of bibliography quality, identity separation, voyage/fate narrative structure, and where the linked institutional records can substantiate the guide. This is not an external link crawl, exhaustive technical fact-check, or independent inspection of original register volumes.

| Guide | Evidence review | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SS *Iberia* (1954) | Four ship-specific P&O Heritage Collection publicity artifacts document the distinct launch, scheduled Australia service and later cruise-marketing phases, in addition to a specialist chronology. Modern print-shop descriptions must not be confused with independent voyage logs. | Retain. |
| SS *Champlain* (1932) | French Lines & Compagnies archives identify an original 1939 voyage report; its collections separately document construction photographs. GG Archives complements institutional holdings. | Retain. |
| SS *Lafayette* | BnF ship-identity authority record and French Lines vessel register distinguish the named career stages *Mexique*, *Île de Cuba*, and *Lafayette*. These are identity aids, not primary proof of every date/tonnage. | Retain. |
| SS *France* (1962) | French Lines historical brochure, Loire-Atlantique departmental archives timeline, and Mariners' Museum collection artifact form a useful independent institutional source set. Phase-specific measures and later *Norway* career should remain distinguished. | Retain. |
| SS *Cap Arcona* (1927) | Neuengamme Concentration Camp Memorial's ship-specific research and U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum background are appropriate specialist sources for the May 1945 catastrophe. The human tragedy demands particular caution about casualty estimates and eyewitness testimony. | Retain. |

**Disposition:** No file changes to the five guides were justified from this selective review. Future targeted research: obtain dated P&O *Iberia* technical or itinerary records; inspect the 1939 *Champlain* report rather than infer its contents; and triangulate *Cap Arcona* fatality totals from survivor, memorial, and contemporary records. Existing Key Facts and narrative untouched. This report is an audit trail, not a certificate of historical accuracy.
