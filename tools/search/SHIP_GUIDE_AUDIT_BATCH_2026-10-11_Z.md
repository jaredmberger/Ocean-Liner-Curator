# Ship-guide sourcing audit — batch 36 (11 October 2026)

Preservation-first, selected-claims review of five existing guides. This record distinguishes source metadata, corroboration and unresolved primary-document work. It is **not** a complete historical verification, sitewide link crawl or check of every figure.

| Guide | Selected check | Result and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ships/ss-president-roosevelt.html` | Civilian name sequence and later USS *Joseph T. Dickman* service | Naval History and Heritage Command DANFS and the U.S. Coast Guard Historian independently institutionalize 1922 name changes, War Department takeover in October 1940, 10 June 1941 Navy commission and 7 March 1946 decommission. Added Coast Guard historical record to bibliography; did not reinterpret January 1922 completion as February 1922 delivery. Precise shipyard milestones remain a separate possible check. |
| `ships/rms-orcades.html` (1937) | Identity and October 1942 loss | Existing ship-specific uboat.net entry supports 10 October 1942 loss to U-172, 45 deaths, 1,022 survivors and 23,456 tons in that database. The guide explicitly preserves its reported 23,445–23,456 GRT range. Retain; original Board of Trade inquiry or contemporary rescue reports would improve independent corroboration. |
| `ships/mv-jean-laborde.html` | 1940–48 wartime/disposal milestones | Existing specialist Messageries Maritimes account describes 25 June 1940 immobilization, use as a training ship after return to Marseille, 19 August 1944 burning at Caronte and 1948 scrapping. Retain. That source is a historical reconstruction, not an inspected original log or yard demolition certificate. |
| `ships/ss-athos-ii.html` | Wartime source specificity and disputed launch year | Existing references distinguish U.S. MARAD vessel documentation, NHHC convoy history and U.S. Army historical discussion of an inconclusive sabotage inquiry. Guide already identifies the 1925/1926 launch-date disagreement, rather than suppressing it. Retain. An original AG Weser launch record is needed to close the date question. |
| `ships/rms-rangitata.html` | 1929 service and 1949 re-engining source trail | Existing National Library of New Zealand and *Papers Past* references, including contemporary 1929 and 1949 items, are specifically identified; wartime museum and Australian naval-history sources are also cited. Retain. This is a targeted revisit of a previously recorded ship, **not** new full-guide coverage; original builder machinery drawings and dated ownership/registry entries were not checked. |

**Disposition:** One bibliography-only improvement to `ships/ss-president-roosevelt.html`; four other guides unchanged. No Key Facts, narrative chronology, headings, page metadata, image, script or navigation edits. Existing uncertainty remains explicit.
