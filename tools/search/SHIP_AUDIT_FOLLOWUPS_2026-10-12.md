# Archive audit follow-ups — 12 October 2026

Preservation-first reconciliation of findings from rapid ship-guide audits. These are editorial clarifications and source attribution repairs; none constitute full historical certification.

## Resolved in this pull request

- **Orontes guide distinction:** `ships/rms-orontes.html` documents the **1902 Orient Line vessel**, while `ships/ss-orontes.html` documents the **separate 1929 Orient Line vessel**. They are **not duplicates**. Reciprocal contextual links are added at the start of both guides. Keep both indexed and canonical.
- **SS Minnesota:** removed a generic Wayback Machine homepage falsely labeled as a naval record. The actual, directly linked Naval History and Heritage Command USS *Troy* source remains intact.
- **SS Mayflower:** replaced generic GG Archives homepage falsely labeled as a Dominion Line passenger list with its actual Dominion Line passenger-list collection; explicitly labeled as a **line-level index**, not a ship-specific Mayflower manifest.
- **SS Otranto:** removed a Norway Heritage homepage falsely labeled a ship-specific source. Other relevant 1918 loss, contemporary naval, wreck and construction citations remain.
- **SS Resolute:** corrected the Marhisdata URL from `/schip&id=1089` to `/schip?id=1089`. Marhisdata ID 1089 documents *Brabantia* → *Resolute* → *Lombardia*, including the 1914 original launch and 1920 delivery.

## Intentional distinctions not requiring a merger

- **George Washington:** two guides cover different operational eras of the **same ship**, the NDL original period and subsequent American / United States Lines service. They remain separate at the owner's direction. The United States Lines presentation is addressed by PR #477.
- **Tall Ships Guide:** `ships/tall-ships-guide.html` is a **thematic reference**, not a unique ocean-liner vessel. Preserve it, but do not conflate 371 indexed guide paths with 371 distinct ocean-liner hulls. Its presence in the current coverage-report denominator is a scope/inventory matter, not an erroneous ship fact. Structural exclusion from the ship index requires a separate decision concerning sitemap, internal links, and coverage generation.
- **Principessa Mafalda casualties:** a future deeper historical verification candidate; do not amend casualty figures speculatively.

## Preservation and verification limits

No ship biography, date, tonnage, image, metadata, or historic Key Facts changed. Scope is limited to clearly identified page context and bibliography links.
