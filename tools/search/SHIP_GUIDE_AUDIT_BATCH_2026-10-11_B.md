# Ship guide audit — five-guide batch (11 October 2026, second batch)

Read-only preservation-first editorial audit. Inspected each current guide's chronology, Key Facts, narrative structure, and bibliography. Figures were sampled against independent historical sources, not exhaustively certified. No ship-guide content has been shortened or silently overwritten.

| Guide | Assessment | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ships/ms-kungsholm-1928.html` | Substantive treatment of the Swedish American Line years and three later identities; keeps route-service and cruise configurations distinct. | Registry tonnage and the later-name transition years should be reconciled against dated registers if future revisions are made. |
| `ships/ms-saturnia.html` | Distinguishes original Cosulich service, 1935–36 modernization, transport/hospital service, and postwar migration. NavSource confirms the Frances Y. Slanger hospital conversion in 1945 and return to Italian government in November 1946. | Preserve original-versus-refit figures, check the 1927 maiden voyage against contemporary timetables. |
| `ships/ms-vera-cruz.html` | Full South America and Angola service context and Portuguese postwar fleet-renewal discussion; references Portuguese museum and periodical sources. | Verify dates and passenger capacities against those primary records during a deeper check. |
| `ships/qsmv-dominion-monarch.html` | Thorough liner/refrigerated cargo/troopship separation. A shipbuilder-focused record lists **26,463 GRT** and **508 first-class berths**, while the guide currently has **27,155 GRT** and **525 first-class passengers**. These differences warrant registry/phase reconciliation before any numeric edit; they are not automatically historical errors. | High-priority fact-verification candidate. Review Swan Hunter records, dated registers and whether values reflect changes of measurement/configuration. |
| `ships/rms-alaunia.html` | Launch 9 June 1913 and loss 19 October 1916 agree with a specialist Cunard shipwreck history; loss attributed to UC-16 mine, with two lives lost. Civilian versus military service is clearly differentiated. | Check the original sailing dates via contemporary Cunard sources; current guide says only 'late 1913'. |

## Decision

No numerical changes proposed in this batch: the most material apparent conflict (*Dominion Monarch* capacity and GRT) is a **research question** without definitive phase-controlled documentary resolution. Prefer preserving verified context and recording the outstanding discrepancy rather than replacing one plausible figure with another.

Audit scope does not include a full deployed image crawl, every external link, or independent certification of every ship datum. No SEO-led rewriting.
