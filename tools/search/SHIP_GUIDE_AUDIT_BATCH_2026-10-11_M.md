# Ship-guide audit — batch 23 (11 October 2026)

**Five previously unaudited guides:** `ships/ss-rotterdam-1959.html`, `ships/rms-aquitania.html`, `ships/ss-france-1912.html`, `ships/rms-mauretania.html`, and `ships/ss-paris.html`.

This batch is a selective review of existing bibliographies, vessel identities and narrative organization; it does **not** represent exhaustive fact-checking, verification of live external URLs, or independent source-by-source validation.

| Guide | Existing evidence assessment | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SS *Rotterdam* (1959) | Preservation project's official history and Holland America Line history provide direct institutional sourcing for operation and preservation. Distinct from the 1908 and later *Rotterdam* vessels. | Retain. |
| RMS *Aquitania* | Multiple Royal Museums Greenwich dated photographic and as-fitted plan collection entries are provided. Civilian, hospital ship, troopship and postwar phases are separated. | Retain. |
| SS *France* (1912) | French Lines & Compagnies ship-specific history and company vessel-register link already support the identity of *France II*. Distinct from the 1962 *France*. | Retain. |
| RMS *Mauretania* (1907) | Royal Museums Greenwich's dated 1908 main-deck plan (CWS0043) and 1907 contemporary artwork give object-level source support for design and image interpretation. | Retain. |
| SS *Paris* (1921) | Bibliography responsibly distinguishes a 1972 commemorative image from a contemporary service chronology and refers to a press-photo collection for the 1939 fire. Stronger institutional archival voyage or disaster documentation would help substantiate detailed operational claims. | **Follow-up research candidate**, no unsubstantiated addition. |

No source-supported historical corrections identified during this limited review; all guides deliberately remain unchanged. A broad web search for a suitable institutional *Paris* source did not produce a clearly matching record, so no questionable link was introduced.

These notes are audit leads, not proof of historical accuracy. Prioritize original records for a disputed date, tonnage, voyage, or casualty claim before making edits.
