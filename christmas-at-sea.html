A Christmas at Sea

The traditions, celebrations, and quiet moments of the holiday season aboard the great ocean liners.

Christmas on the ocean routes

A holiday crossing brought the familiar calendar into an unfamiliar setting: a ship at sea, its passengers and crew far from home. Menus, photographs, passenger accounts, and contemporary reports can offer glimpses of how the season was marked aboard individual vessels.

This page is the starting point for a developing seasonal collection. Specific shipboard customs and stories will be added only as supporting historical evidence is reviewed.

From decoration to documentation

Future additions will distinguish contemporary records from later recollections, identify vessels and dates where possible, and acknowledge gaps in the surviving record.
