# Editorial source-review ledger

Last updated: 2026-10-11 (UTC).

This is a working record of **selected claims reviewed**, not a certification of whole guides or historical truth. It helps editors avoid repeating the same checks and find unresolved questions.

## How to use this ledger

Before choosing a review batch, search for the guide and claim here, then read the linked PR. A reviewed source label does not mean that the guide's chronology, technical figures or narrative were checked. A retained claim is not necessarily independently verified.

The initial entries below are backfilled from merged PR descriptions and the reviews documented in those PRs. Sources were **not re-fetched for this ledger**. PR links preserve the original review scope, limitations and changes. Dates in the tables are PR merge dates in UTC, not inferred research dates. This is a partial historical backfill; absence from the ledger means **not recorded here**, not never reviewed.

Disposition terms:

- **Corrected**: a documented editorial correction was merged.
- **Clarified**: milestone, attribution, object type or uncertainty was made more precise.
- **Sample agreed**: the explicitly listed claims agreed with sampled references; the guide is not certified.
- **Retained / unresolved**: evidence did not establish a correction or fully verify the claim.
- **Source reference only**: the review improved bibliography links or labels without establishing all historical claims.

## Recent claim-level reviews

### Minnewaska (1908) route, capacity and salvage (pending PR #376)

Malta versus Saloniki voyage-destination descriptions conflict; first-class berths and troop complement must not be conflated; 1917 salvage work is separately documented from the asserted 1918 disposal. Primary movement, accommodation and demolition records remain required. [PR #375](https://github.com/jaredmberger/Ocean-Liner-Curator/pull/375) merged 2026-10-11 UTC.


### Rangitiki in Convoy HX 84 (pending PR #375)

Arnold Hague's reconstructed convoy list identifies Rangitiki's formation position and vice-commodore role. A dated 20 November 1940 witness interview transcript adds detail on the raider's movements without proof of damage to Rangitiki. Original action reports, technical and disposal documentation remain outstanding. [PR #374](https://github.com/jaredmberger/Ocean-Liner-Curator/pull/374) merged 2026-10-11 UTC.


### Orion 1947 service resumption (pending PR #374)

Museums Victoria's original passenger diary catalogue supports a February 25 Tilbury departure and March 27 Melbourne arrival; contemporary March 27 press confirms the vessel was already en route. The alternative February 27 date and the exact yard refit finish remain open. [PR #373](https://github.com/jaredmberger/Ocean-Liner-Curator/pull/373) merged 2026-10-11 UTC.


### Empress of Britain (1930) launch versus trials (pending PR #372)

Contemporary reporting distinguishes the June 1930 launch from the vessel leaving Clydebank for trials in April 1931; no original formal builder acceptance date has yet been verified. [PR #371](https://github.com/jaredmberger/Ocean-Liner-Curator/pull/371) merged 2026-10-11 UTC.


### Stockholm / Astoria 1948 departure and 2025 recycling milestones (pending PR #371)

Swedish America Line historical chronology records the 21 February 1948 maiden voyage. Galloo, the actual recycler, records the 3–4 July 2025 final tow and a projected ~12-month dismantling process; the ship was not entirely scrapped in July solely because it arrived then. The actual final closeout remains unverified. [PR #370](https://github.com/jaredmberger/Ocean-Liner-Curator/pull/370) merged 2026-10-11 UTC.


### Conte di Savoia dated wartime transition (pending PR #370)

A U.S. embassy dispatch dated 16 May 1940 documents a Genoa–New York civilian sailing the previous evening. Delivery 29 November 1932 appears in a ship-specific register and the inaugural departure 30 November in a regional historical archive. Neither proves a wartime requisition order. [PR #369](https://github.com/jaredmberger/Ocean-Liner-Curator/pull/369) merged 2026-10-11 UTC.


### Deutschland (1900) and Celtic (1901) catalogue specificity (pending PR #369)

The existing GG Archives Deutschland link now explicitly distinguishes the 1900 vessel from its 1923 namesake. National Museums NI catalogue item HOYFM.HW.H699 provides a ship-identified original Harland & Wolff photograph with dated Celtic launch and delivery metadata. The 20,904 catalogue tonnage and 20,902.07 guide tonnage must not be silently conflated. [PR #368](https://github.com/jaredmberger/Ocean-Liner-Curator/pull/368) merged 2026-10-11 UTC.


### Orcades (1948) air-conditioning timeline (PR #366 merged)

## Updating this record

When editing an **Open questions** row, explicitly review its **Research status** and **Research route**, update both summary tallies, and move fully resolved questions out of the active queue only after recording the evidence and limitations. The status applies to the *whole row*; do not call an entry resolved while another question in that row remains open.


Append or refine an entry in the same editorial PR as a new review:

1. Identify the exact guide path and claims sampled.
2. Record the disposition, source URL and page/object/record locator.
3. Note access limitations, conflicting definitions and anything left unresolved.
4. Link the editorial PR. For a pending PR, say pending; add the confirmed merge date after merge.
5. Close or supersede only the specific open question answered by new evidence. Keep prior PR links so the decision remains traceable.

Do not derive coverage percentages from this partial ledger or count source-reference improvements as complete historical audits.
